# E03 Terms and pilot counts

The minimal pilot terms, the seeker's acceptance of them at payment, and the numbers the founders read at the two reviews (backlog: E03, issue #9). Each state below is one story in **Product > Screens > E03 Terms and pilot counts**.

Sources: the backlog, the booking spec (Grill 9, the pilot-count query), the vault spec, and decisions D08, D15, D16, D17, D18 and D19. The screens are examples built from the design system, with example data and no routing.

## Steps of the flow

1. The pilot terms are written for review and checked by the verifier before the first payment (E03-T01, E03-T02).
2. A seeker reads the terms and accepts them at payment, with a box that is never ticked in advance (E03-S01).
3. At the two reviews, on 30 November and 14 December 2026, the founders read the pilot counts and decide whether to stop or revise (E03-S03, D08).
4. Thirty days after the pilot ends, session text and notes are deleted (E03-T03). This is a job and has no screen.
