# Content

How the product talks. The interface is in English. The tone follows the references: facts in plain words, calm, concrete (R01, R02; the words are a proposal until confirmed).

## Tone

**Three sentences the product says**

1. "Pay 25 EUR and send." The verb, the amount, the result.
2. "If the mentor does not accept or does not show up, you are refunded." The condition and what follows (R04).
3. "Active today. Not a promise of reply time." A fact, and what it does not promise.

**Three sentences the product does not say**

1. "Top mentor ★ 4.9." A rating or a ranking (N02, A03, A05).
2. "Oops! Something went wrong 🙈" An error that says neither what happened nor what to do, with an emoji (A01, proposal P3: no emoji in the interface).
3. "Unlock the power of AI to supercharge your career." Sales language (A05: "to jest coś sprzedażowego?").

## Buttons: a verb and an object

| Write | Not | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Pay 25 EUR and send | Submit | It says what will happen and what it costs |
| Accept, Decline (the question from Sam) | OK, Yes | The name of the button says the action. A row adds the object for assistive technology |
| Take down note | Yes, delete | The object is named |
| Publish note | Continue | The user knows what is published |
| Save draft | Save | The user knows it is not published |
| Try again | Retry | Plain words |

Source: Confirm destructive action pattern, Button page. The rule "a verb and an object" is a proposal supported by the Confirm pattern.

## Errors: what happened and what to do

| Write | Not |
|---|---|
| "The payment failed. Your card was not charged. Check the card details and try again." | "Payment error" |
| "You already have a session at that time. Choose a later start or decline this one." | "Something went wrong" |
| "Give the note a title." | "Invalid input" |
| "The notice changed. Read it again before you publish." | "Conflict (409)" |

An error about money always says whether money was taken. No blame, no exclamation marks. Source: WCAG 3.3.3, Form pattern.

## Empty states: why it is empty and the next step

| Where | Write |
|---|---|
| Seeker's bookings | "No questions yet. Pick a mentor and ask your first question." with a button "Find a mentor" |
| Mentor's bookings | "No questions waiting. When someone pays for a question to you, it appears here." |
| Notes | "No notes yet. Write down what you explain again and again. A note feeds AI answers only after you publish it." |
| Answers to review | "Nothing to review. When an answer cites one of your notes, it appears here and we email you." |
| Credits | "No credits yet. You earn a share when a vault answer that cites your note is released." |

No illustration, no icon tile: the words and the next step are the content (A01).

## Dates, times, money and numbers

| What | Format | Example | In code | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Date | Day, short month, year | 3 Nov 2026 | `formatDate(date, timeZone)` | Proposal |
| Time of day | 24 hours | 14:25 | `formatClock(date, timeZone)` | Proposal |
| Time zone | Pass it explicitly where two people share a time | `timeZone="Europe/Warsaw"` | the `timeZone` prop | Proposal |
| Length of time | Hours and minutes | 1 h 20 min | `formatDuration(minutes)` | Proposal |
| Price in a sentence or on a button | Whole amounts without decimals, the currency code after the amount | 25 EUR | `formatPrice(amount, currency)` | Proposal |
| Price in a table or a ledger | Always two decimals, so digits line up | 25.00 EUR | `formatPrice(amount, currency, 'exact')` | Proposal |
| Counts | A number and a unit, in tabular figures | 3 people | `tabular-nums` | Proposal |

No reference gives a format. All of the above are proposals, and the owner can change them in one place (`lib/format.ts`). The server and the client use the same functions. A price is stored in minor units (`priceMinor`) and never as a decimal number.
