# PilotCountsTable

## 1. What it is for

The numbers the founders read at the two reviews (E03-S03): each criterion of the pilot with its value, the provisional threshold of the decision record, the review date and where the criterion stands.

<Canvas of={PilotCountsTableStories.AtTheFirstReview} />

## 2. When to use it

- The operator's page of pilot counts, on 30 November and 14 December 2026 (D08).

## 3. When not to use it, and what to use instead

| Situation | Use instead | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A long, paginated list of records | `DataTable` | This table is a handful of criteria, read whole |
| A mentor's earnings | `CreditsPanel` | A different audience and a ledger |
| A chart | Not built | The criteria are single numbers with a threshold |

## 4. Anatomy

1. **A table** with column headers: Criterion, Value, Provisional threshold, Review, Status (R01.3).
2. **What is counted**: live payments only, a vault answer counts when it is released.
3. **A reminder** that the thresholds are provisional targets with no baseline.
4. **When the numbers were computed**, when known.

## 5. Variants

| Status of a row | Words | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| `open` | Window still open | neutral |
| `triggered` | Stop-or-revise condition met | danger |
| `clear` | Condition not met | neutral |

The criteria, thresholds and review dates are those of decision D08. They are passed in, so editing a threshold in the decision record does not change the component.

## 7. Behavior and accessibility

- **A real table** with a caption for screen readers and column headers.
- **Values** are right-aligned with tabular figures.
- **The status is words**, and the tone only supports it.
- **The table states facts.** Whether to stop or revise is the founders' decision, and the component never says so.
- **Test payments are not counted** and a refunded vault purchase is not counted (booking spec, Grill 9, and the vault spec).

## 8. Content

- **Value:** the number with its unit: "3 people", "74 min". Before the first answer: "No answer yet".
- **Threshold:** as the condition of the record: "fewer than 5 people within 4 weeks of the start".
- **The numbers in the stories are examples**, not pilot results. The pilot has not started.

## 12. Do and don't

| | Example | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Do | The condition next to the number | The reader sees what the number is compared with |
| Don't | "Pilot failed" | The decision belongs to the founders |
| Do | Say test payments are not counted | The number means real money |
| Don't | A green or red cell with no words | Colour alone |
