# BookingStatus

## 1. What it is for

Says where a booking stands, in words, from the point of view of the person reading. It also says where the money stands after a decline, an expiry or a no-show (`RefundStatusLine`).

<Canvas of={BookingStatusStories.ReadByTheSeeker} />

## 2. When to use it

- In `BookingRow`, next to the other person's name.
- In the heading of a booking's detail view.

## 3. When not to use it, and what to use instead

| Situation | Use instead | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A status that is not a booking's | `StatusBadge` directly | This component knows the booking statuses |
| A long explanation of what happens next | Text under the badge, or an `Alert` | A badge is one to three words |

## 4. Anatomy

1. **Badge**: a `StatusBadge` with the status text.
2. **Refund line** (optional): plain text next to the badge.

## 5. Variants

The wording depends on who reads it.

| Status | The seeker reads | The mentor reads |
|---|---|---|
| `pending_payment` | Awaiting payment | Awaiting payment |
| `payment_failed` | Payment failed (danger) | Payment failed |
| `paid` | Waiting for the mentor | Awaiting your decision (active) |
| `accepted` | Accepted (active) | Accepted (active) |
| `declined` | Declined (danger) | Declined |
| `expired` | Not answered in time (danger) | Not answered in time |

A negative outcome is shown to the seeker as a problem and to the mentor, who caused it, as a plain fact. The tone assignment is a **proposal**: the success and warning tones are not defined yet (conflict K3, see POSTEP.md).

<Canvas of={BookingStatusStories.ReadByTheMentor} />

## 6. States

| State | What the user sees | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Each status | A badge with words and a tone | WCAG 1.4.1 (words, not colour alone) |
| Refund `pending_approval` | "Refund pending approval" | Booking spec: refunds that wait for the operator |
| Refund `processing` | "Refund processing" | Booking spec: the gateway call is running |
| Refund `refunded` | "Refunded" | Booking spec |
| Refund `none` | Nothing is rendered | |

<Canvas of={BookingStatusStories.WhereTheMoneyStands} />

## 7. Behavior and accessibility

- The meaning is in the words. The tone only supports it.
- It is plain text, not a live region: a status that changes is announced by the screen that changes it.

## 8. Content

- **From the reader's side:** "Waiting for the mentor", "Awaiting your decision". Never the internal name (`paid`).
- **Refund:** says where the money stands. It never says "soon".

## 9. Responsiveness

The badge and the refund line wrap onto two lines when the space is narrow.

## 11. Code

## 12. Do and don't

| | Example | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Do | "Awaiting your decision" for the mentor | It says what the reader must do |
| Don't | "paid" for the mentor | It names the payment, not the decision the mentor has to make |
| Do | "Refund processing" | Tells where the money is |
| Don't | A green dot for "refunded" | Colour alone |

## 13. Related

- **Confused with:** `StatusBadge` (one word, any status).
- **Used in:** `BookingRow`, Patterns: Paid action flow, Detail view.
