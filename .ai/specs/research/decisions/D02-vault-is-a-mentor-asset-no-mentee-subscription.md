# D02 — In the MVP the vault is a mentor-side asset; no mentee subscription
- Date, owner: 2026-10-25, founder A (Oliwia), confirmed in the discovery session
- Context and the options weighed: (1) vault sold to the seeker as a separate subscription; (2) vault as the mentor's resource that produces the answer inside the session or the per-question price. The agent recommended (2); the owner accepted it.
- Decision and why: (2). Of four seekers interviewed, three declined a subscription (working developer, fintech backend developer, platform developer) and two declined saved answers (fintech, platform); the freelancer was not asked about either. Accepting the recommendation does not confirm that seekers want vault answers (A01).
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: the board goals "30% of active mentees on a vault subscription" and "subscription revenue overtakes session commission" are deferred, not dropped. Revisit when seekers show repeat use of vault answers.
- Status: active
