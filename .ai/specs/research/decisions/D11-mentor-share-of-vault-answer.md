# D11 — The mentor receives 80% of the price paid for a vault answer, split equally among cited mentors
- Date, owner: 2026-10-25, founder A (Oliwia), confirmed in the discovery session; answers Q10; supplements D09
- Context and the options weighed: D09 fixed a share and left the amount open. The agent proposed an example and the owner accepted it. The only reference in the material is the benchmark check of Topmate (a commission of 10% on an expert's own link and 20% on customers acquired through its marketplace) and the board's revenue frame of 15 to 20% on sessions, which is the team's position, not evidence; it supports that product's observed fee, not what our mentors or seekers accept. No mentor was asked about a share (A04, collection plan). The price of a vault answer is not set (Q05); payment processing fees are not decided.
- Decision and why: 80% of the price the seeker pays goes to the mentor, 20% to the platform; when several mentors' notes feed one answer, the mentors' part is split equally among the mentors of the cited notes. The percentage is a provisional choice with no empirical basis; the owner's stated reason was the choice itself.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: review at the end of the A01 test window (D08) or when a mentor declines because of the share. Payment processing fees, whether the share is computed before or after them, and the price itself stay open (Q05). Equal split is the owner's choice for the several-notes case; nothing shows it is fair to mentors.
