## 🎯 Scope and limits

This was a static local prototype walk, not a running product. Each persona started in an isolated browser session at the invitation. P01 and P03 proceeded to the profile screen, then proposed Back; the browser was not sent back because the walk ended at that state. P02 and P04 deferred at the invitation. The subsequent profile validation, availability, review, completion, reset, and recovery states were not walked by this panel. No credentials or real/personal profile data were entered. The prototype's context describes its values and slots as fictitious, but personas did not choose to enter them. Screenshots are actual captures of observed states.

## 🔍 Barriers that repeated

No barrier that stopped the task was established across both runs. Two personas proposed Back at the profile step, but neither tested whether they could continue with a fictitious public-work link, and the source interview does not establish that the mentor would refuse one. The two run instances share the same single interview source, so their similar reaction is not independent real-person replication.

## 📋 Missing cases that repeated

### Required public-work link: optionality and visibility are unclear to the interview-grounded persona

[SYNTHETIC]

Weight: 2 observations across 2 runs; spread: ±0; personas: P01 (run 1), P03 (run 2). Both are fresh synthetic instances grounded in the same August interview, not independent interviewees. Touches prototype assumption A-P1 and the mentor-list decision D04; it does not establish a contradiction.

At the profile screen, the accessible form marks “Link to public work” required. P01's fast reaction was “I’d need to think carefully about what I share”; P03 was cautious and questioned what must be shared. The interview passage says “my GitHub is my profile” and that public ratings are not valued, but it does not say the mentor refuses a public-work link. Neither persona submitted the form. The repeated issue is therefore a question about requiredness and audience visibility, not evidence that the flow blocks mentors.

**Real check:** recruit invited mentors who answer recurring questions with private notes, including one who currently shares public work through a personal link. Ask them to complete the profile step as they normally would. At the public-work link field, ask them to say who they understand the audience to be and show what they do if they choose not to share a link. Observe whether they can understand the audience and proceed. This informs D04 and A04; no specific recruitment count is established by the brief.
